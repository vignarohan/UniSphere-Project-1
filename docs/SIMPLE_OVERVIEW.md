# UniSphere in Simple Terms
UniSphere puts students' academic tasks into one organised portal and gives lecturers an overview of their classes.
Students check attendance, schedules, results, CGPA, assignments and payment dues. They open notes and recordings, submit work, catch up on missed lessons, practise using Quiz Me and join lecturer-hosted quizzes.
Lecturers upload teaching materials, set assignments, mark work, record attendance and filter students who may need support. Admins manage users, academic setup, schedules, sample fees and support requests.
A small robot button opens the AI assistant. It can find personal pending work, locate a missed lesson and explain the uploaded material. It cannot invent missing records or access another student's private information.
Everyone can submit a category-based ticket to an eligible receiver. The receiver gets a portal notification and email link and replies inside the portal.
This FYP is a working demonstration with fictional data. Payments are simulated. Catch-up completion and practice scores remain separate from official attendance and grades.
